# Lexical definitions — Feelings Wheel (126 unique words)

**What this is:** dictionary/lexical baseline definitions for every feeling word in the wheel,
sourced from licensing-clear lexica so the wording can be adapted freely. This is the *plain
meaning* layer; the therapeutic warmth/framing lives in `therapeutic.md`, and both are merged
into the final app corpus in `reconciled.md`.

**Sources & licenses used:**
- **WordNet 3.0** (Princeton) — permissive WordNet license — short glosses, primary source.
- **Wiktionary** — CC BY-SA — used for words WordNet lacked or where the emotion sense was clearer.
- **GCIDE / Webster 1913 / Century Dictionary** — Public domain — older but clean glosses.
- All consulted via Wordnik aggregation. Proprietary dictionaries were consulted only to
  sanity-check meaning, never reproduced.

Sense selection: where a word has multiple senses, the **emotion/feeling** sense was chosen and
noted (e.g. "Content" adj. satisfied; "Aroused" non-sexual stirred/agitated; "Bad" feeling
unwell; "Cheeky" playful-impudent; "Free" social/unconstrained).

**Coverage: 126 / 126. Gaps: 0.** (Duplicated wheel words — Disappointed, Embarrassed, Inferior,
Overwhelmed — are defined once each.)
