# Reconciled definitions — Feelings Wheel (126 unique words)

**What this is:** the FINAL, voice-consistent definition corpus for the Interactive Feelings
Wheel, merging three sources into one tight set:

- `lexical.md` — plain dictionary meaning (accuracy, sense selection).
- `therapeutic.md` — warm, validating framing + the disambiguations that matter for a therapy tool.
- the existing `FEELINGS_DATA.definitions` in `feelings-data.js` — good prior wording preserved
  where it was already strong; replaced where research was clearly better or too clinical/circular.

Each word has two fields:
- **standard** — one warm, precise sentence (~15–28 words) that names what the feeling *is* and
  often what it signals or arises from.
- **simplified** — a genuinely plainer, shorter version (~grade-6, ~8–16 words) for the app's
  simplified mode. Not a truncation of the standard — plainer words throughout.

Register is deliberately gentle, second-person-friendly, non-clinical, and non-pathologizing. The
older `feelings-data.js` entries leaned diagnostic ("An emotional and physiological response…");
those were rewritten to warm the voice and remove circularity. **Ready for human review before it
goes into `feelings-data.js`.**

The **notes** column records: source lean, any disambiguation honored, and whether the entry was
kept, improved, or newly written vs. the existing file.

## Needs review

None. All 126 words have confident entries. A few placement/sense calls to keep an eye on during
approval (carried over from the source files, not blockers):

- **Aroused** and **Cheeky** — written strictly to the wheel's playful-*Happy* sense. These are the
  two most likely to be misread by users, so the disambiguation is baked into the wording ("stirred
  up and full of lively energy"; "playfully bold and a bit mischievous").
- **Busy** — framed as a *felt* over-fullness rather than a neutral report of a full calendar,
  matching therapeutic.md; confirm this matches the intended branch placement.
