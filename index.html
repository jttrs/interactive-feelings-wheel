How to use the wheel

During a session

Using the wheel

  • Choose a feeling to add it to the list with its meaning. Choose it again to remove it.
  • Tap a name in the list to show or hide its meaning.
  • Drag or scroll to turn the wheel.
  • Press and hold a small word to see it larger (or point at it with a mouse).

Views

Full wheel
The default — the whole spectrum of feelings.
Simplified
For younger clients: hides the outer ring and uses simpler meanings. Anything chosen there stays chosen.
Focused
Starts with the core feelings and opens each ring as a feeling is chosen, to explore one step at a time.

Focused and Simplified can be on together: the simpler rings, opened one step at a time. The current view is shown beside “Views” at the bottom of the panel.

Keyboard

Tab Reach the wheel
←→ Move around a ring (or spin the wheel when no feeling has focus)
↓↑ Step out to more specific feelings / back in
[] Previous / next family (or Page Up / Down)
Enter Choose the feeling (or Space)
? Show these keys beside the word you’re on

Setting up

Start a session in a view

The page address keeps the current view, so you can bookmark it. For a younger client, open ?view=simplified; also ?view=focused and &panel=hidden.

Shortcuts

S Simplified view
F Focused view
P Hide or show this panel
R Reset — clear chosen feelings
F11 Fullscreen (Esc to leave)
