How are you feeling?

Choose any word on the wheel that feels close. Pick as many as fit — there are no wrong answers.

Drag the wheel to turn it. Keyboard: Tab to the wheel, ? for keys.

How to use the wheel

Explore feelings

Choose any feeling on the wheel to add it to your list, with what it means.

Pick as many as fit. To remove one, choose it on the wheel again — or press Reset to start over.

In your list, tap a feeling’s name to show or hide its meaning.

Words too small to read? Press and hold one to see it larger — slide away to cancel.

Rotate the wheel

Drag it, scroll over it, or press the ←→ arrow keys to spin it — hold to keep spinning.

Move with the keyboard

Press Tab to reach the wheel, then ←→ to move around a ring, ↓ to step out to more specific feelings and ↑ to step back in.

[] or Page Up / Page Down jump to the next family; Enter or Space chooses a feeling.

Press ? on the wheel to show these keys beside the word you’re on.

View and actions

  • Focused view starts with the core feelings and opens each ring as a feeling is chosen, so you can explore one step at a time. The full wheel stays in view behind it. F
  • Simplified view is for younger clients: it hides the outer ring and uses simpler meanings. Anything chosen there stays chosen. S
  • Fullscreen gives the wheel the whole screen; press Esc to leave. F11
  • Reset clears your chosen feelings and re-centres the wheel. R
  • Hide panel tucks this panel away to give the wheel more room. P
