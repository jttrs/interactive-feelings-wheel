How to use the wheel

Explore feelings

  • Tap / click any emotion to select it and read its meaning.
  • Select several to compare — tap a tile’s ✕ (or the emotion again) to remove it.

Rotate the wheel

  • Drag it, scroll over it, or focus a wedge and use the arrow keys.

View options

  • Reset clears your selections and re-centres the wheel R
  • Fullscreen gives the wheel the whole screen F11
  • Simplified view hides the outer ring for a calmer wheel S
  • Collapse this panel any time P
